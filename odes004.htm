Apocrypha: The Odes Chapter 4: The Prayer of Habakkuk

1 A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet.

2 O Lord, I have heard thy report, and was afraid; I considered thy works, and was astonished. In the midst of two living creatures thou shalt be known; when the years draw nigh, thou shalt be acknowledged; when the time is come, thou shalt be manifested; when my soul is troubled, in wrath thou shalt remember mercy.

3 God shall come from Thaiman, and the Holy One from the mount of Pharan, with thick shade, densely wooded. Pause. His virtue covered the heavens, and the earth was full of his praise.

4 And his brightness shall be as the light; horns are in his hands, and he hath established a mighty love of his strength.

5 Before his face shall go the word, and his feet shall go forth in sandals.

6 He stood, and the earth shook; he beheld, and the nations melted; the mountains were violently shattered, the everlasting hills melted away.

7 His everlasting goings I beheld in the place of labours; the tents of the Ethiopians shall tremble, and the tabernacles of the land of Madiam.

8 Wast thou angry, O Lord, with the rivers? or was thy wrath against the rivers? or thy surge against the sea? for thou shalt mount upon thy horses, and thy chariots are salvation.

9 Thou shalt surely bend thy bow against the sceptres, saith the Lord. Pause. The land shall be cleft with rivers.

10 The peoples shall see thee, and travail; thou scatterest the waters of thy goings; the deep uttered her voice, and lifted up her form on high.

11 The sun and the moon stood still in their course; at the light of thy darts they shall go, at the shining of thy glittering spear.

12 With thy threatening thou shalt diminish the earth, and in wrath thou shalt break the nations.

13 Thou wentest forth for the salvation of thy people, to save thine anointed ones; thou didst cast death upon the heads of the lawless; thou didst raise up bonds as high as the neck. Pause.

14 Thou didst cut asunder the heads of the mighty in amazement; they shall tremble therein; they shall open their bridles as a poor man eating secretly.

15 And thou didst bring thy horses into the sea, troubling many waters.

16 I kept watch, and my belly trembled at the voice of the prayer of my lips; and trembling entered into my bones, and my frame was troubled under me; I will rest in the day of my affliction, that I may go up to the people of my sojourning.

17 For the fig tree shall not bear fruit, and there shall be no produce on the vines; the labour of the olive shall fail, and the fields shall yield no food; the sheep are gone from the fold, and there are no oxen in the stalls, as an atonement for them.

18 Yet I will rejoice in the Lord, I will joy in God my Saviour.

19 The Lord my God is my strength, and he will set my feet unto the end; he will cause me to tread upon the high places, that I may overcome by his song.
