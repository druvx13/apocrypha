Apocrypha: The Odes Chapter 14: A Morning Hymn

1 Glory to God in the highest,

2 And on earth peace,

3 Good will toward men.

4 We praise thee,

5 We bless thee,

6 We worship thee,

7 We glorify thee,

8 We give thanks to thee

9 For thy great glory.

10 O Lord King,

11 Heavenly,

12 God the Father Almighty;

13 O Lord, the only-begotten Son,

14 Jesus Christ;

15 And the Holy Spirit.

16 O Lord God,

17 Lamb of God,

18 Son of the Father,

19 That takest away the sins of the world,

20 Have mercy upon us.

21 Thou that takest away the sins of the world,

22 Receive our prayer.

23 Thou that sittest at the right hand of the Father,

24 Have mercy upon us.

25 For thou only art holy;

26 Thou only art the Lord;

27 Jesus Christ,

28 To the glory of God the Father. Amen.

29 Every day will I bless thee;

30 And I will praise thy name for ever,

31 And for ever and ever.

32 Vouchsafe, O Lord, this day also

33 To keep us without sin.

34 Blessed art thou, O Lord God of our fathers,

35 And praised and glorified be thy name for ever. Amen.

36 Blessed art thou, O Lord: teach me thy statutes.

37 Blessed art thou, O Lord: teach me thy statutes.

38 Blessed art thou, O Lord: teach me thy statutes.

39 Lord, thou hast been our refuge from generation to generation.

40 I said, Lord, have mercy upon me;

41 Heal my soul, for I have sinned against thee.

42 Lord, I have fled unto thee;

43 Teach me to do thy will, for thou art my God.

44 For with thee is the fountain of life;

45 In thy light shall we see light.

46 O continue thy lovingkindness unto them that know thee.
