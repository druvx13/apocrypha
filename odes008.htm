Apocrypha: The Odes Chapter 8: The Hymn of the Three Children

52 Blessed art thou, O Lord God of our fathers, and praised and exalted above all for ever: and blessed is the holy name of thy glory, and exceedingly praised and exalted above all for ever.

53 Blessed art thou in the temple of thy holy glory, and exceeding hymn-worthy and exceeding glorious for ever.

54 Blessed art thou that beholdest the deeps, and sittest upon the cherubim, and exceedingly praised and exalted above all for ever.

55 Blessed art thou on the throne of thy kingdom, and exceeding hymn-worthy and exalted above all for ever.

56 Blessed art thou in the firmament of heaven, and exceeding hymn-worthy and exceeding glorious for ever.

57 Bless ye the Lord, all ye works of the Lord: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

58 Bless ye the Lord, ye angels of the Lord: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

59 Bless ye the Lord, O ye heavens: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

60 Bless ye the Lord, all ye waters that are above the heavens: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

61 Bless ye the Lord, all ye powers of the Lord: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

62 Bless ye the Lord, O sun and moon: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

63 Bless ye the Lord, ye stars of heaven: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

64 Bless ye the Lord, every shower and dew: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

65 Bless ye the Lord, all ye winds: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

66 Bless ye the Lord, O fire and heat: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

67 Bless ye the Lord, O cold and fervent heat: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

68 Bless ye the Lord, O dews and snowfalls: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

69 Bless ye the Lord, O frost and cold: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

70 Bless ye the Lord, O hoarfrosts and snows: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

71 Bless ye the Lord, O nights and days: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

72 Bless ye the Lord, O light and darkness: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

73 Bless ye the Lord, O lightnings and clouds: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

74 Let the earth bless the Lord: let her praise and exalt him above all for ever.

75 Bless ye the Lord, O mountains and hills: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

76 Bless ye the Lord, all things that spring up in the earth: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

77 Bless ye the Lord, O fountains: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

78 Bless ye the Lord, O seas and rivers: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

79 Bless ye the Lord, O whales, and all that move in the waters: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

80 Bless ye the Lord, all ye fowls of the heaven: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

81 Bless ye the Lord, O beasts and all cattle: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

82 Bless ye the Lord, O ye sons of men: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

83 Let Israel bless the Lord: let him praise and exalt him above all for ever.

84 Bless ye the Lord, O ye priests of the Lord: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

85 Bless ye the Lord, O ye servants of the Lord: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

86 Bless ye the Lord, O ye spirits and souls of the righteous: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

87 Bless ye the Lord, O ye holy and humble of heart: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.

88 Bless ye the Lord, O Ananiah, Azariah, Mishael: praise him and exalt him above all for ever.
