Apocrypha: The Odes Chapter 2: The Ode of Moses in Deuteronomy

1 Give ear, O ye heavens, and I will speak; and let the earth hear the words of my mouth.

2 Let my declaration be awaited as the rain, and let my words descend as the dew, as the shower upon the tender grass, and as the snowfall upon the herb.

3 For I have called upon the name of the Lord: ascribe ye greatness unto our God.

4 God, his works are true, and all his ways are judgment: a faithful God, and without iniquity, just and holy is the Lord.

5 They have sinned, the blameworthy children are not his; a generation perverse and crooked.

6 Do ye thus requite the Lord, O foolish people and unwise? Is not he thy father that hath possessed thee, and made thee, and created thee?

7 Remember the days of old, understand the years of generation upon generation: ask thy father, and he will declare unto thee; thy elders, and they will tell thee.

8 When the Most High divided the nations, when he scattered the sons of Adam, he set the bounds of the peoples according to the number of the angels of God.

9 And the Lord's portion was his people Jacob, the line of his inheritance was Israel.

10 He sustained him in the wilderness, in the thirst of burning heat, in a waterless land; he compassed him about, and instructed him, and kept him as the apple of his eye.

11 As an eagle spreadeth over his nest, and fluttereth over his young, stretching abroad his wings, he received them, and took them up upon his back.

12 The Lord alone did lead them, and there was no strange god with him.

13 He made them to ride on the high places of the earth, and fed them with the increase of the fields; and he made them to suck honey out of the rock, and oil out of the hard rock;

14 Butter of kine, and milk of sheep, with fat of lambs and rams, sons of bulls and goats, with the fat of kidneys of wheat; and they drank wine, the blood of the grape.

15 So Jacob did eat and was filled, and the beloved kicked; he waxed fat, he grew thick, he spread abroad; and he forsook God that made him, and departed from God his Saviour.

16 They provoked me to jealousy with strange gods, with abominations they embittered me.

17 They sacrificed unto demons, and not to God; unto gods whom they knew not, new and fresh ones came, whom their fathers feared not.

18 Thou hast forsaken God that begat thee, and hast forgotten God that nourisheth thee.

19 And the Lord saw, and was jealous, and was provoked to wrath because of the anger of his sons and daughters.

20 And he said, I will turn away my face from them, and I will show what shall befall them at the last; for it is a froward generation, children in whom is no faith.

21 They have moved me to jealousy with that which is no god, they have provoked me to anger with their idols; and I will move them to jealousy with that which is no people, with a foolish nation will I provoke them to anger.

22 For a fire is kindled in my wrath, it shall burn down to Hades beneath, and shall devour the earth with her increase, and set on fire the foundations of the mountains.

23 I will gather evils upon them, and spend mine arrows upon them.

24 Pining with hunger, and consumed by the devouring of birds, and with incurable destruction of the back: I will send forth the teeth of wild beasts upon them, with the fury of things that creep upon the ground.

25 Without, the sword shall bereave them of children, and terror out of the chambers; young man and virgin, the suckling with the man of advanced age.

26 I said, I will scatter them abroad, I will make their remembrance to cease from among men;

27 Were it not for the wrath of the enemy, lest their adversaries should deem it strange, and lest they should say, Our hand is high, and not the Lord hath done all this.

28 For they are a nation void of counsel, neither is there understanding in them.

29 They considered not to understand these things; let them receive them for the time to come.

30 How should one chase a thousand, and two put ten thousand to flight, except their God had sold them, and the Lord had delivered them up?

31 For their gods are not as our God; but our enemies are fools.

32 For their vine is of the vine of Sodom, and their branches out of Gomorrah; their grape is a grape of gall, their clusters are bitterness unto them.

33 Their wine is the poison of dragons, and the incurable venom of asps.

34 Lo, is not this laid up in store with me, and sealed up among my treasures?

35 In the day of vengeance I will recompense, in the season when their foot shall slide; for the day of their destruction is at hand, and the things prepared for you make haste.

36 For the Lord shall judge his people, and shall be moved with compassion for his servants; for he saw them enfeebled, and failing in the calamity, and utterly helpless.

37 And the Lord said, Where are their gods, the rock in whom they trusted,

38 The fat of whose sacrifices ye did eat, and drank the wine of their drink offerings? Let them rise up and help you, and be your protection.

39 See, see now that I, even I, am he, and there is no god beside me; I kill, and I make alive; I wound, and I heal; neither is there any that can deliver out of my hand.

40 For I lift up my hand to heaven, and swear by my right hand, and say, I live for ever.

41 For I will sharpen my sword as lightning, and mine hand shall take hold on judgment; I will render vengeance to mine enemies, and to them that hate me will I recompense.

42 I will make mine arrows drunken with blood, and my sword shall devour flesh, with the blood of the wounded and of the captives, from the head of the princes of the enemies.

43 Rejoice, O ye heavens, together with him, and let all the angels of God worship him; rejoice, O ye nations, with his people, and let all the sons of God strengthen themselves in him; for he avengeth the blood of his sons, and will avenge, and will render vengeance to his enemies, and will recompense them that hate him, and the Lord will cleanse the land of his people.
